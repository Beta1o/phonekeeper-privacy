PhoneKeeper — Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

1. What the app reads

With your permission the app reads, on your phone only:

  • Text messages (SMS and MMS) — to show reports of who you text, bills and money messages, and to export them.
  • Call history — to show call reports and to export it.
  • Contacts — to show names in reports, find duplicates, fix names and numbers, and back them up.

2. What the app writes

Only when you start it: messages and call history imported from your own export file (the app asks to be the default SMS app for this), changes to contacts that you review and confirm first, and files you export or back up into Downloads/PhoneKeeper.

3. What leaves your phone

Your messages, calls and contacts never leave the phone unless you choose to. The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. It does not upload, share or sell any data. A file only leaves the phone when you tap Email, Drive or Share and pick where it goes.

Optional tips are paid through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; the app never sees your card or payment details. Google's billing library, which the app includes for tips, may send purchase information to Google under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). This is why the app lists the Internet permission.

4. Storage and deletion

Reports are worked out in memory. Your settings (tags, spellings, categories) are stored in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Files you exported stay in Downloads until you delete them.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

6. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is shown in the app and on the store page.
